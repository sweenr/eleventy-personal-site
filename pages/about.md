Hi, my name is Richard! I'm a freelance software developer based in the Jackson, MS metro area. I've been developing software professionally for over 15 years, starting as a summer intern writing a web crawler in Java to search for and index web services. I married the love of my life [Kayla](https://www.kaylasween.com) in 2016 and we have two Jack Russell terriers, Captain and Piper.

Previously, I was the Head of Engineering at [HourWork](https://hourwork.sprockets.ai), a B2B SaaS company that helps hourly employers, primarily in the quick-service restaurant industry, recruit and retain employees. Our biggest project there by far was a complete system rebuild, replacing the existing tech debt-ladened backend and no-code frontend with a new full stack Javascript/Typescript system running an Express API and Lambdas on the backend and React on the frontend. We finished the project in nine months and released it with no major customer-facing issues.

Before that, I was as an Engineering Manager and software engineer at Kopis Mobile, a small company based in Mississippi building apps and hardware for the military and first responders. In my time there, I worked a number of projects, including a rugged video sharing and streaming platform. I also led the technical implementation of a web platform to store, organize, and share safety and security best practices for sports and entertainment venues.

Previously, I worked on software development at the [Center for Advanced Vehicular Systems](https://www.cavs.msstate.edu) in the Human Factors group. My role was developing software tools to assist researchers at the Center, including a few projects in VR using Unity and the HTC Vive. I also spend a few summers working at the [Naval Research Lab](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/Our-Work/Field-Sites/NRL-South/) at Stennis Space Center, mostly doing Java development.

I received my Master's degree in Computer Science in December 2012, from Mississippi State, with a focus on Software Engineering. I did my undergraduate work in Computer Science and Software Engineering, obtaining dual Bachelor's degrees from Mississippi State in May, 2011.

In my free time, I like to tinker with home automation using [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io), play video games, and travel with Kayla. I also enjoy learning new things and working on side projects.
